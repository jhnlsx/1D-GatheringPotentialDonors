WHO WE ARE

About Our System

♥

Our Purpose

BloodLink aims to provide an organized platform for gathering potential blood donor information and presenting blood donation requests in one accessible place.

◎

Our Goal

Our goal is to help improve communication between potential donors and people seeking blood donors through a simple and user-friendly system.

♡

Our Commitment

We aim to promote awareness about blood donation, encourage community participation, and support efforts that may help save lives.

🎯
OUR MISSION

Making Connections Easier

To develop an accessible and organized system that gathers potential donor information and helps users view blood requests, supporting awareness and community participation in blood donation.

🌟
OUR VISION

A Community That Cares

To help build a community where people are more aware of blood donation and where potential donors can be easier to identify and contact through an organized system.

HOW IT WORKS

Three Simple Steps
